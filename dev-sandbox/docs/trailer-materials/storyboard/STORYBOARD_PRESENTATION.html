MISC. PALACE

60 Second Trailer — Visual Storyboard

Production Screenplay & Shot Breakdown

SHOT 1 0:00–0:03 (3 sec)

Vignette Fade-In + German Notices Viewer

Start from black. Over 1-2 seconds, a vignette circle opens/iris reveals the warm, cozy 3D room. Isometric angle showing fireplace (warm amber glow, lower-left), armchair, furniture, rug. Slow gentle pan/orbit.

Then CUT TO: Mid-shot of German sitting in the armchair, well-dressed, silver streak in hair, unlit bubble pipe, reading comic book. As dialogue begins, he glances up and notices the viewer—slight knowing smile.

"Welcome to the miscellaneous manor. It can get pretty scary out there, so much doom filled, endless scrolling…"
📷 Camera: Isometric wide establishing → mid-shot of German
🔊 Audio: As vignette opens, fireplace ambience fades in. Music bed enters low. German's voiceover begins.
Production Notes: Room ambience (fireplace crackle) is critical from fade-in. German's delivery: natural, warm, conversational. No stage voice. Capture room tone separately.
SHOT 2 0:03–0:10 (7 sec)

Comic Swirls Into Preview Thumbnail

Cut back to 3D room view (isometric). German's hand visible holding comic book. Comic lifts from hands, swirls in mid-air with gentle rotation (~1-1.5 sec). As it swirls, morphs into a floating rectangular preview card showing nostalgic internet content (retro music player UI, webtoon, early 2000s website, etc.). Camera slow zoom-in on thumbnail over ~4 seconds, depth of field keeps thumbnail sharp, background softens.

"…We think the internet could use some warmth and a little charm."
📷 Camera: Isometric room → slow zoom-in on thumbnail (3-4 sec)
🔊 Audio: Music swells gently. Optional brief audio snippet of the media being referenced.
VFX Note: Comic swirl/morph is key transition. Keep magical but not cartoonish (~1.5 sec morph time). Thumbnail content should be recognizable/nostalgic (music UI, old webtoon, etc.).
SHOT 3 0:10–0:18 (8 sec)

Contemplative Preview Card

Close-up of the preview card. It floats centered, framed with a soft glow. A quiet moment to take it in — nostalgic, inviting. The camera holds on this single moment, letting the user absorb the significance of what they're seeing.

"Someone sent me this song, and it reminded me of a comic book I once had."
📷 Camera: Close-up, centered on floating preview card. Subtle ambient light and glow.
🔊 Audio: Music continues softly. Contemplative, wistful mood.
Purpose: Emotional beat. Connect viewer to the personal, nostalgic feeling of discovering old treasures. This is the "why" moment.
SHOT 4 0:18–0:27 (9 sec)

Full Room Reveal with Icon Overlays

Camera pans/rotates upward and around the room over 9 seconds, showing vertical scope: walls, upper shelves, ceiling, architectural depth. Objects visible throughout: books, vinyl records, TV with knobs/antenna, plants, sleeping cat, desk with lamp, artwork on walls. As camera pans, small semi-transparent icons fade in above object clusters (2-3 sec per icon set).

🎵 Music note (gold/amber) above audio items
🎪 Toy/circus (warm red) above entertainment
✏️ Pencil/tool (soft blue) above work/productive
📖 Book icon (russet) above reading materials
"I've kept all my digital treasures, all in one place."
📷 Camera: Smooth pan/orbit upward (~30-45° rotation). Isometric view throughout.
🔊 Audio: Music builds gently. Ambient room tone continues.
Icon Note: Icons are subtle, semi-transparent, ~2-3% of screen scale. Appear/fade naturally (~2 sec per group). Category clusters: music, books, entertainment, cozy items.
SHOT 5 0:27–0:35 (8 sec)

Montage — Objects Fly Into Place

Fast-paced montage sequence. 4-5 object placements, each 0.75-1.5 seconds. Objects picked up and snapped onto shelves/surfaces with satisfying bounce/settle:

📗 Book lands on shelf → *thump*
💿 Vinyl spins onto turntable → *snick*
🎙️ Cassette lands neatly → *tap*
😴 Cat settles in bed → *whoosh*
🎧 Headphones on hook → *click*
"What would your miscellaneous palace look like?"
📷 Camera: Wide room view with slight zoom-ins per placement for emphasis. Quick cuts for variety.
🔊 Audio: Music takes full stage. Soft tactile SFX per placement (thunk/snap/whoosh). NO UI beeps.
Pacing: SNAPPY. Each placement ~0.75-1.5 sec. Convey: "Organizing is fun and satisfying." Objects fully visible, readable, not silhouettes. Note: Dialogue may span between Shots 5 and 6.
SHOT 5b 0:35–0:40 (5 sec)

Zoomed-In Objects with Preview Cards

Zoomed-in detail shot showing three interactive objects on a shelf: sleeping cat, CRT TV, yo-yo. Preview cards float above each when clicked — music player UI above cat, play button above TV, retro game above yo-yo. Items remain static on shelf display, showing what happens when users interact with the collection.

📷 Camera: Close-up, isolated shelf detail. Each object and preview clearly visible.
🔊 Audio: Music continues. Optional subtle click/hover feedback SFX.
Purpose: Show the interactive moment—users can click/hover on objects to see previews. Bridge between placement (Shot 5) and magic organization (Shot 6).
SHOT 6 0:40–0:53 (13 sec)

Magic Organization (Concept Sequence)

Overhead/god's-eye isometric view, looking down ~45°. Cardboard box (open, full of mixed objects) sits center of room. Magic wand appears and waves over box. Objects swirl up from box in curved, elegant flight paths (not straight lines). Each object floats to its thematic zone: music items group left-center, work items right, entertainment lower-left, cozy items center. Objects settle with gentle hover-and-descend, soft glow fading as they land.

⭐ ON-SCREEN TEXT: "CONCEPT SEQUENCE"
📷 Camera: Fixed overhead god's-eye isometric. Subtle orbit optional (~5° only).
🔊 Audio: Music swells to crescendo. Magical sparkle/swirl/whoosh SFX. Soft settling sounds. Optional magical chime.
Note: Dialogue from Shot 5 ("What would your manor look like?") may continue through this sequence. This is aspirational concept work, not a shipped feature.

Phone/Web View Perspective

Show mobile phone (portrait orientation) OR desktop monitor/laptop with Misc. Palace room displayed on screen. Room shown at reduced scale in responsive layout. Isometric camera angle visible on-screen. UI elements visible (buttons, controls) around room. Room readable but distant on small screen.

"Venture out and see what others have to share" (begins here)
📷 Camera: Wide shot showing device taking up most of frame. Screen content clearly visible.
🔊 Audio: German's voiceover continues. Music steady bed.
Capture: Real mobile web capture of dev-sandbox. Show responsive design working. Interaction should feel natural.
SHOT 7b 0:53–0:58 (5 sec)

VR First-Person POV

First-person headset perspective, seated eye-level in armchair. Head turns smoothly across ~30-40° total: look left (see side table, lamp, bookshelf), look forward (fireplace large and warm), look right (another wall, more objects). Slow natural head movements (~10-15° per direction). Objects at HUMAN SCALE (large, close, real). Fireplace looms large and enveloping. Warm ambient lighting makes you feel PRESENT in the space.

📷 Camera: Pure first-person headset POV. No visible hands or interface (optional subtle reticle only).
🔊 Audio: Music steady. Ambient room sounds: fireplace crackle, subtle presence tone.
⚠️ CRITICAL - VR CAPTURE: Must be REAL VR headset footage (Quest/similar). Do NOT fake or composite. This is the authentic differentiator between web and VR. Non-negotiable for credibility.
SHOT 7c 0:58–1:02 (4 sec)

Third-Person Avatar View

Isometric 3/4 third-person camera showing avatar in room center/center-right. Avatar is stylized character or simple humanoid (consistent with voxel aesthetic). Avatar is RELAXED & CHILLED: sitting or standing casually, headphones on, body language comfortable and at ease. Room visible all around avatar. Avatar may have gentle sway/bob to music (subtle). Warm lighting throughout.

📷 Camera: Isometric 3/4 third-person. Avatar visible center frame, room surrounding. Static or slow orbit (~5°).
🔊 Audio: Music continues. Optional very subtle audio from avatar (faint music leak if headphones on).
Capture: Dev-sandbox avatar model + room, OR composited real person into 3D space. Goal: show ownership, comfort, and joy in personal space.
SHOT 8 1:02–1:14 (12 sec)

Neighborhood Exploration

Avatar walks toward exit of main room (takes ~2-3 sec). Camera pulls back and up (zoom-out, tilt), revealing wider neighborhood street/cul-de-sac. Stylized voxel buildings visible (each represents a person's room). Winding path connecting buildings. Warm golden-hour lighting. Quick montage pans showing different themed rooms (each 1.5-2 sec):

🌿 Plant Room - greens, bright, botanical
🎵 Music Room - colorful records, warm, eclectic
🎨 Creative Room - organized desk, tools, focused
🏠 Cozy Room - personal touches, cat visible, warm
📷 Camera: Wide room shot → zoom-out/pull-back revealing neighborhood → smooth pans along path showing 3-4 different rooms.
🔊 Audio: Music gentle or shifts to neighborhood theme. Ambient: wind, distant sounds from rooms (very subtle). Optional footsteps.
Labels (Optional): "German's Room," "Matt's Room," etc. visible on buildings. Neighborhood should feel cozy & inviting, not industrial. Each room distinct—shows personalization & variety.
SHOT 9 — END CARD 1:14–1:18 (4 sec)

End Card with Call-to-Action

Clean title card over warm background (either final neighborhood shot faded, or warm solid color like burnt orange/amber). Centered text hierarchy:

Primary: "MISC. MANOR" or "PALACE" (large, bold, white/cream)
Tagline: "A room for your favorite things" (medium, white/cream)
CTA: "Play now" / "Try the demo" (medium-small, warm accent color)
URL/QR: Real, tested destination (small, bottom-right or center)
📷 Camera: Static end card, high contrast text, readable on mobile & desktop.
🔊 Audio: Music reaches gentle, satisfying resolution. Quiet closure.
⚠️ CRITICAL: URL/QR must be REAL & TESTED. NO placeholder text. Must be functional, tested destination before export. Hold for 4 seconds (readable time on phone). No animation or movement on end card.
