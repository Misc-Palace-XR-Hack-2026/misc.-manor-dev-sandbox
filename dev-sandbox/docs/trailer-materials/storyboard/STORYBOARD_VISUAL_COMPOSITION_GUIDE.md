## SHOT 1: VIGNETTE FADE-IN + GERMAN NOTICES VIEWER
**Duration:** 0:00–0:03 (3 seconds)
**Aspect Ratio:** 16:9

### COMPOSITION & LAYOUT

**Part A (0:00–0:02): Vignette Fade-In**
- Start with BLACK screen
- Gradually open vignette iris/fade revealing the warm 3D room behind
- Vignette circle opens from center outward over ~2 seconds
- Within the vignette: cozy isometric 3D room becomes visible

**Room Interior (visible through vignette):**
- Camera angle: Isometric 3/4 view, slightly from above (approx 30° tilt down)
- Key elements visible in frame:
  - Fireplace: glowing warm amber/orange, slightly lower-left of center
  - Armchair: brown/warm upholstered, positioned center-left facing slightly toward viewer
  - Side table: small wooden table next to armchair
  - Rug: warm burnt-orange/rust colored, anchoring the scene
  - Wall: soft neutral gray-beige with warm orange-amber firelight casting
  - Furniture silhouettes: soft shadows on walls from firelight
  - General feel: cozy, lived-in, warm lighting from fireplace

**Lighting:**
- Key light from lower-left (fireplace glow)
- Soft ambient from above
- Vignette darkening around edges creates focus
- Overall warm color cast: ambers, warm grays, rust tones

**Part B (0:02–0:03): Cut to German**
- HARD CUT to mid-shot of German in armchair
- German visible from roughly waist up
- He's sitting comfortably, leaning back slightly in the armchair
- Appearance: well-dressed, silver streak in dark hair, unlit bubble pipe in mouth
- He's reading a comic book (held in both hands at chest level)
- Expression: absorbed in reading, warm ambient lighting modeling his face
- Background: fireplace visible slightly out of focus behind/beside him
- Same warm lighting as room (continuous from Part A)

## SHOT 2: COMIC SWIRLS INTO PREVIEW THUMBNAIL
**Duration:** 0:03–0:10 (7 seconds)
**Aspect Ratio:** 16:9

### COMPOSITION & LAYOUT

**Scene Setup:**
- Camera angle: Isometric 3/4 room view (return to full room perspective)
- German's figure visible lower-left of frame, seated in armchair
- German's hand/arm visible in frame holding the comic book

**Action Sequence:**

1. **Comic Book in Hand (first 1 sec):**
   - German holds comic book at chest/center level
   - Book visible in foreground, clear readable cover illustration
   - Room and fireplace visible in background

2. **Swirl & Morph Animation (1–2.5 sec):**
   - Comic book lifts from German's hands, rising toward center-right of frame
   - Book begins rotating/spinning in mid-air (gentle 360° rotation)
   - As it spins, the rectangular book shape MORPHS into a floating rectangular preview card/thumbnail
   - Preview thumbnail shape becomes clear: ~4:3 or 16:9 rectangle, semi-transparent border
   - Morphing should feel magical but not cartoonish (smooth interpolation, ~1.5 sec)

3. **Preview Thumbnail Content (2.5–7 sec):**
   - Thumbnail floats center-right of frame
   - Preview displays: nostalgic internet content (pick one or show transition):
     - Option A: Retro music player UI (80s/90s style, with play button, equalizer bars)
     - Option B: Webtoon panel or comic excerpt (indie art style)
     - Option C: Retro website screenshot (early 2000s aesthetic)
   - Thumbnail should have a subtle glow or border to show it's a preview
   - German still visible in lower-left, looking at the floating preview

4. **Camera Zoom-In (2.5–7 sec):**
   - Slow push-in on the thumbnail over ~4 seconds
   - Depth of field: thumbnail remains sharp, background (German/room) softly blurred
   - Zoom should be gentle and smooth, not jarring

**Color Palette:**
- Warm room tones (fireplace amber) in background
- Thumbnail content: bright, nostalgic colors (reds, yellows, blues from era being referenced)
- Smooth warm-to-content color transition as zoom occurs

## SHOT 4: FULL ROOM REVEAL WITH ICONS
**Duration:** 0:18–0:27 (9 seconds)
**Aspect Ratio:** 16:9

### COMPOSITION & LAYOUT

**Camera:**
- Start with established room view from Shot 3 end
- Slow pan/rotation upward and around room (orbit ~30–45° over 9 seconds)
- Reveal vertical scope of room: walls, upper shelves, ceiling, architectural features

**Room Content (visible as camera pans):**
- Objects distributed across shelves, tables, floor, nooks:
  - Books on shelves (vertical stacks)
  - Vinyl records/cassettes
  - CRT TV with knobs/antenna
  - Potted plant (tall, leafy green)
  - Sleeping cat (curled up in corner or bed)
  - Desk with lamp and work items
  - Artwork or framed items on walls
  - Other decorative/personal objects scattered throughout
  - Overall: curated but lived-in, not sterile

**Icon Overlay System:**
- As camera pans, small icons fade in above/near object clusters (~2 sec per group)
- Icons are subtle, semi-transparent, 2-3% scale of screen:
  - **Music note / Treble clef** (gold/amber color): above music items (records, cassettes, headphones)
  - **Toy/Circus tent icon** (warm red): above entertainment items (TV, games, fun objects)
  - **Pencil / Tool icon** (soft blue): above work/productive items (desk, books, organized supplies)
  - **Book icon** (russet brown): above reading materials

**Lighting:**
- Fireplace key light still visible (lower-left warmth)
- Additional ambient lighting from upper sources (natural-looking ceiling light)
- Warm overall, inviting

**Camera Motion:**
- Panning upward reveals ceiling/upper walls
- Gentle orbit left-to-right or right-to-left (choose one direction for flow)
- Motion is smooth, not jerky; appreciative pace (not rushed)

**End State:**
- Camera settles on a nice wide establishing shot showing ~60% of room contents
- All icon types visible
- Room feels populated and organized but personal

## SHOT 5: OBJECT MONTAGE — FLYING INTO PLACE
**Duration:** 0:27–0:35 (8 seconds)
**Aspect Ratio:** 16:9

### COMPOSITION & LAYOUT

**Montage Series (Quick Cuts):**
Show 4–5 individual object placements, each taking 1–2 seconds. Cuts are quick and snappy. Camera angle: wide room view with slight zoom-ins on key objects for emphasis.

**Placement 1: Book on Shelf**
- Book appears on-screen or in hand
- Flies/snaps up and onto a shelf in one smooth motion (~0.5–0.75 sec flight)
- Lands with satisfying *thunk* SFX
- Brief settle animation (slight bounce, then rest)
- Visible in left-center portion of frame on a wooden shelf

**Placement 2: Vinyl Record on Turntable**
- Vinyl record spins into view from upper-center
- Descends in a spiral/curved path toward turntable (visible in frame)
- Lands and settles on turntable with soft *snick* sound
- Turntable is center-right of frame; record glints in warm light

**Placement 3: Cassette Tape on Small Shelf**
- Cassette appears from above-right
- Glides down and settles onto a small shelf/table surface
- Landing SFX: *tap* (slightly sharper than book, metal-on-wood feel)
- Positioned upper-right portion of frame

**Placement 4: Cat in Bed/Cozy Spot**
- Sleeping cat appears or flies from off-screen
- Descends smoothly into a cozy nook or small bed
- Cat settles with a soft *whoosh* and curl animation
- Positioned lower-center or lower-left, clearly cozy and restful
- Cat is distinct and recognizable (orange/brown color, clearly "sleeping")

**Placement 5 (Optional): Headphones on Hook**
- Headphones loop from above, settling onto a small hook/stand
- Landing with soft *click*
- Positioned upper-center or upper-right

**SFX Throughout:**
- Each placement has a distinct but warm, tactile sound
- *Thunk*, *snick*, *tap*, *whoosh*, *click* — no digital beeps or artificial SFX
- Keep sounds punchy and satisfying (not too loud, not too quiet)

**Lighting & Color:**
- Maintain warm room lighting throughout
- Objects are fully visible and readable (no silhouettes)
- Each placement is clearly visible and distinguished
- Camera may shift angle slightly between placements for variety (one from left, next from right)

**Pacing:**
- SNAPPY. Each placement ~0.75–1.5 sec per object
- No drag or slowness
- Convey: "organizing is fun and satisfying"

## SHOT 6: MAGIC ORGANIZATION (CONCEPT SEQUENCE)
**Duration:** 0:35–0:48 (13 seconds)
**Aspect Ratio:** 16:9

### COMPOSITION & LAYOUT

**Camera Angle:**
- Overhead/god's-eye isometric view, looking down ~45° angle
- Can show room from above, with clear floor layout and all walls visible
- Camera stays fixed or orbits very slowly (subtle motion only)

**Initial Setup (0:35–0:40, ~5 sec):**
- **Cardboard box:** Prominent in center of room floor
  - Box is open, full of mixed objects (books, records, toys, etc. visible inside)
  - Box should read as "unpacked, unsorted items"
  - Positioned dead center or slightly off-center (leave space for action)

- **Room:** Same warm cozy space as before
  - Shelves and surfaces visible but mostly empty or partially populated
  - Space inviting and ready for organization

**Magic Wand Animation (0:40–0:48, ~8 sec):**

1. **Wand Appears & Activates (0:40–0:42):**
   - Magic wand appears from upper-center of frame (off-screen to on-screen)
   - Wand is simple/stylized: stick with star or swirl tip
   - Warm glowing aura around wand (soft gold/amber sparkle)

2. **Objects Fly Out (0:42–0:45):**
   - As wand waves/points, objects swirl up from the box one by one
   - Each object follows a curved, elegant flight path (not straight lines)
   - Curves arc upward, then drift/float toward different zones of the room
   - Objects visible as they fly:
     - Book → curves toward a shelf area
     - Vinyl record → arcs toward turntable/music zone
     - Cassette → floats to music grouping
     - Toy/entertainment item → curves to entertainment zone (left side)
     - Work item (pencil, desk object) → drifts to desk/work zone (right side)
   - Flight pattern shows **thematic organization:** objects group by type as they land

3. **Objects Settle (0:45–0:48):**
   - Each object gently settles into its final position
   - Slight hover-and-descend animation as it lands
   - Subtle glow around each landed object (fading away)
   - Objects are now organized in clusters:
     - **Music corner:** records, cassettes, headphones together (left-center)
     - **Work zone:** desk, books, organized items (right)
     - **Entertainment area:** TV, toys, fun items (left-lower)
     - **Cozy zone:** cat, plants, comfort items (center/upper)

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:**
- **"CONCEPT SEQUENCE"** appears on-screen during this entire sequence (~full 13 sec)
- Text placement: upper-left or lower-left, semi-transparent, readable but not obtrusive
- Font: clean, bold, clear (no script or overly decorative fonts)
- Purpose: makes clear this is aspirational/conceptual, not a shipped automatic feature

**Lighting:**
- Warm firelight throughout
- Optional magical shimmer/sparkle as objects fly (gold/amber particles, subtle)
- Glow around wand
- Soft glow around landing objects (fading)

**Color Palette:**
- Warm amber/gold sparkles for magic effect
- Room remains warm and cozy
- Objects show their true colors (books browns/reds, records colorful, etc.)

**Pacing & Feel:**
- MAGICAL but not cartoonish
- Smooth, satisfying, elegant movements
- Each object flight takes ~1–1.5 sec
- Settling/landing is brief and satisfying
- Total motion should feel like a dance or orchestrated event
- Convey: "This is the dream of effortless organization"

## SHOT 7: MULTI-VIEW SEQUENCE
**Duration:** 0:48–1:02 (14 seconds)
**Aspect Ratio:** 16:9

### COMPOSITION & LAYOUT

**Shot 7a: Phone/Web View (0:48–0:53, ~5 seconds)**

- **Framing:** Show a mobile phone or desktop monitor in-hand or on a surface
  - Option A: Phone held in portrait orientation, screen visible to viewer
  - Option B: Desktop monitor/laptop showing the app
  - Choose one and be consistent

- **App Display:**
  - Misc. Palace room visible on screen in responsive/mobile layout
  - Room shown at reduced scale (smaller viewport than full VR)
  - Isometric camera angle visible on screen
  - UI elements visible: maybe buttons (Edit Room, Visit Neighborhood, etc.) around room view
  - Screen should look crisp and readable

- **Room on Screen:**
  - Cozy room interior visible but at smaller scale
  - Objects visible but distant/small
  - Conveys: "You can see and interact with the room, but it's on a small screen"

- **Lighting:**
  - Device screen is bright/glowing
  - Warm lighting in room interior (visible through screen)
  - Clear, readable display

- **Camera/Perspective:**
  - Wider shot showing the device taking up most of frame
  - Viewer can clearly see the screen content

**Shot 7b: VR First-Person POV (0:53–0:58, ~5 seconds)**

- **Camera Angle:** First-person headset perspective
  - Viewer is sitting in the armchair, looking around
  - Head height: seated eye-level in the chair
  - Initial look: forward or slightly to the left

- **View Around the Room:**
  - **Initial forward look:** Fireplace visible center of view, warm and large
  - **Look left (0:53–0:55):** Rotate head left, see side table with lamp, bookshelf, objects
  - **Look forward/up (0:55–0:56):** Refocus forward, maybe look slightly up to see upper shelves/wall art
  - **Look right (0:56–0:58):** Rotate head right, see another wall, window perhaps, more objects

- **Head Movement:**
  - Total head rotation ~30–40° across the 5 seconds
  - Movement is smooth and natural (not jarring or nauseating)
  - Roughly 10–15° per side

- **Objects & Lighting:**
  - Objects are now at HUMAN SCALE (large, close, real)
  - Fireplace is large and warm in vision
  - Objects are detailed and recognizable at this scale
  - Warm enveloping lighting makes you feel present in the space
  - Sense: "I'm IN this room, not looking at it on a screen"

- **Camera/Perspective:**
  - Pure first-person, no visible hands or interface
  - Minimal UI (maybe crosshair or subtle reticle, optional)
  - Full immersion into the space

**Shot 7c: Third-Person Avatar View (0:58–1:02, ~4 seconds)**

- **Camera Angle:** Isometric or 3/4 third-person view
  - Camera positioned to show the avatar in the room from a comfortable viewing distance
  - Avatar visible in frame center or center-right
  - Room visible all around the avatar

- **Avatar:**
  - Stylized character or simple humanoid figure (consistent with room's voxel aesthetic, optional)
  - Avatar is RELAXED and CHILLED:
    - Sitting or standing casually
    - Headphones on (visible over avatar's "head")
    - Body language: comfortable, at ease, vibing
    - Optional: subtle animation of avatar bobbing to music or swaying gently

- **Room Around Avatar:**
  - Cozy room visible all around
  - Objects, fireplace, shelves all visible in context
  - Warm lighting
  - Sense: "This is someone's personal space; they own it and love it"

- **Lighting:**
  - Warm overall
  - Avatar is clearly visible and readable
  - Soft shadows showing depth

- **Pacing:**
  - Static or slowly orbiting camera (very subtle, ~5° rotation)
  - Avatar remains relatively still (maybe gentle sway)
  - Convey: peaceful, content, "at home"

## SHOT 8: NEIGHBORHOOD EXPLORATION
**Duration:** 1:02–1:14 (12 seconds)
**Aspect Ratio:** 16:9

### COMPOSITION & LAYOUT

**Part A: Avatar Exits Main Room (1:02–1:08, ~6 seconds)**

- **Initial Framing:** Full room view (isometric), similar to end of Shot 7c
- **Avatar Action:**
  - Avatar stands up or turns toward a doorway/exit
  - Walks forward toward the edge of the room (takes ~2–3 sec)
  - Movement is casual and exploratory

- **Camera Motion:**
  - Wide shot of room initially
  - As avatar approaches exit, camera **pulls back and up** (zoom-out and tilt)
  - Revealing perspective shifts from inside-the-room to outside-and-above
  - By 1:06–1:08, a wider neighborhood view becomes visible

- **Neighborhood Reveal:**
  - **Cooky cul-de-sac or neighborhood street** becomes visible
  - Stylized voxel buildings visible (low-poly, geometric, cozy aesthetic)
  - Each building represents a **separate person's room**
  - Winding path or street connecting buildings
  - Warm lighting (sunset/golden hour feel, or consistent warm ambience)
  - Buildings show variety in color and visual style
  - Some windows/openings visible in buildings, showing room interiors

**Part B: Panning Through Neighborhood (1:08–1:14, ~6 seconds)**

Show quick glimpses of different rooms as camera pans. 3–4 brief cuts/pan angles, each 1.5–2 seconds:

1. **Room 1: Plant/Nature Room**
   - Building with lots of green (plants visible through windows/openings)
   - Bright, botanical aesthetic
   - Color: greens, warm lighting, organic feel
   - Quick glimpse: maybe show avatar walking past, or camera panning to reveal it

2. **Room 2: Music/Vinyl Room**
   - Building with colorful records/music items visible
   - Warm, eclectic aesthetic
   - Color: reds, oranges, yellows (vibrant album art colors)
   - Show: vinyl records on shelves, turntable, music decor

3. **Room 3: Work/Creative Room**
   - Building with organized work setup visible
   - Desk, tools, creative items
   - Color: blues, organized, calm
   - Show: clean shelves, workspace, focused aesthetic

4. **Room 4: Cozy/Personal Room (Optional)**
   - Warm, intimate room with personal touches
   - Similar to main room but with unique personality
   - Show: cat visible, personal photos, lived-in feel

**Camera Movement:**
- Pan along the path, showing multiple rooms in sequence
- Camera moves smoothly left-to-right or in a circuit around the neighborhood
- Avatar may be visible walking, or camera follows behind/ahead of avatar
- Each building gets 1.5–2 sec of screen time

**Lighting & Atmosphere:**
- Warm golden-hour or sunset lighting over neighborhood
- Cozy neighborhood feel (not industrial, not sterile)
- Soft shadows and depth showing buildings are distinct structures
- Path is readable and inviting

**Optional Labels/Text:**
- Room owner names or labels visible: "German's Room," "Matt's Room," "Levi's Room," "Jawen's Room"
- Subtle, small text, not obtrusive
- Adds personalization without cluttering

**SFX:**
- Ambient neighborhood sounds: gentle wind, distant music from other rooms (very subtle)
- Footstep sounds optional
- Keep audio light and peaceful

## SHOT 9: END CARD
**Duration:** 1:14–1:18 (4 seconds)
**Aspect Ratio:** 16:9

### COMPOSITION & LAYOUT

**Background:**
- Option A: Final frame from Shot 8 (neighborhood sunset) fades slightly
- Option B: Fade to solid warm color (burnt orange, warm amber, or deep gray)
- Option C: Freeze-frame of the main cozy room
- Choose one and make it clean and readable
- Background should be warm and inviting, not harsh

**Text & Layout (Center to Upper-Center of Frame):**

**Primary Title (Large, Bold):**
- Text: "MISC. PALACE" or "COZY PALACE"
- Size: Large, fills ~30% of screen width
- Font: Clean, readable, modern (no overly decorative fonts)
- Color: White or warm cream color, high contrast to background
- Positioning: Upper-center or center of frame

**Tagline/Subtitle (Medium, Below Title):**
- Text: "A room for your favorite things" or "Your cozy place on the internet"
- Size: Medium, ~60% of title size
- Font: Same as title or complementary
- Color: Same as title or slightly lighter/warmer
- Positioning: Below title, center

**Call-to-Action (Medium-Small, Below Tagline):**
- Text: "Play now" or "Try the demo" or "Playable now"
- Size: Smaller than tagline
- Font: Clean, slightly bolder for emphasis
- Color: Warm accent color (gold, amber, or warm orange) or same as title
- Positioning: Below tagline, center

**URL/QR Code (Small, Lower-Right or Bottom-Center):**
- **NO PLACEHOLDER TEXT** — must be the real, tested destination URL
- Font: Small, clean, readable on mobile and desktop
- Format options:
  - Direct URL: "misc-palace.com" or similar
  - QR code: ~4–5 cm on-screen size, clear black-and-white
  - Both URL and QR optional (one or both)
- Positioning: Lower-right corner or bottom-center
- Color: High contrast to background

**Overall Layout:**
- All text centered or center-right in frame
- Generous whitespace/breathing room around text
- Hierarchy clear: Title > Tagline > CTA > URL
- Readable on phone screen size
- Clean, elegant, inviting

**Lighting/Background Motion:**
- Background is still (static end card, no moving elements)
- Soft ambient lighting visible in background
- Warm color temperature throughout
- No distracting elements in background

**Duration:**
- 4 seconds total
- Text holds steady (no animation or movement)
- Gives viewer time to read and process CTA
- Allows for music to resolve/end gracefully
