# MISC. PALACE — 60 Second Trailer
## Production Screenplay

**Duration:** ~70-75 seconds (trim as needed in edit)

**Script Base:** g-script-raw-v2.txt

## VISUAL LANGUAGE

- **Style:** Voxelated/low-poly 3D room, warm earth tones, firelight amber
- **Lighting:** Fireplace key light (warm, lower left), soft ambient, vignette edges
- **Objects:** Books, records, TV with knobs, plants, cat, cassette, headphones, rug, armchair, lamp, telescope, robot, vinyl jacket
- **Aesthetic:** Cozy, lived-in, intimate. Isometric 3/4 camera angle.

## PRODUCTION NOTES

- **German's voiceover:** Natural, warm, conversational. No stage voice. Record clean take + room tone.
- **VR footage is critical:** Real headset capture only. Do not fake or composite.
- **Concept sequence (Shot 6):** Must label "CONCEPT SEQUENCE" visibly so it's not mistaken for shipped automation.
- **Music:** Licensed cozy track, levels locked, dips under dialogue, swells during montages.
- **SFX:** Soft placement snaps, magical whooshes, ambient room tone. NO UI beeps.
- **Captions:** All dialogue. Keep readable. No obscuring object labels or concept labels.
- **Color grading:** Warm, cozy throughout. Consistent lighting. No jarring shifts.
- **End card URL/QR:** Must be tested and functional. No placeholder text.
